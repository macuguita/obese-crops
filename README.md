<h1>Obese Crops</h1>
<p>🥕 <b>Bigger crops, that bring a bountiful harvest!</b> 🥕</p>
</center>

This mod adds bigger versions of original crops, they will have a small chance of replace crops when they reach their fully grown stage! We also have scythes, which can be used a weapons, or as a mean to collect your crops and clear grass in your fields.

---

## Features

- **Obese crops**: Bigger versions of obese crops that have a small chance of replacing full grown crops, the more water and light nearby the more chances it has of growing into an obese crop.

![Image displaying all three obese crops: the obese potato, obese carrot and obese beetroot](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/453b49cee13af6a7552af5afc61051d5db470ae6.webp)

- **Scythes**: With the scythes, you can harvest your crops or till the ground with more easy, by default they will do their actions in a 3x3 area, but that can be increased with the bountiful reap enchantment.

![A player holding a netherite scythe](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/7f19287c01eb32804904ecb8d1ff56973f410718.png)

![All the tiers of scythes in the mod: wooden, stone, iron, gold, diamond and netherite](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/e24bf1b682f61869840bcea58866f6274e3e13ea.png)
